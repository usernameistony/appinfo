Katahdin Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

English中文

The short version

Katahdin does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer has no server and cannot see your hikes.

Your hikes

The hikes you log are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for Katahdin, they also sync to your other Apple devices through your own private iCloud database. Apple stores that data under your Apple Account; the developer has no access to it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

You can turn sync off in the Settings app (your name › iCloud). To delete your hikes, delete them in the app, or delete the app and remove its iCloud data under Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Location

Katahdin asks for your location only when you tap the location button on the map, to show where you are on the trail and the nearest shelters. Your location is used on your device for that moment only. It is not saved and not sent to the developer or anyone else.

Maps

The map uses Apple Maps. To draw map tiles, your device requests them from Apple for the area you are viewing, as with any app that shows Apple Maps. The trail line and places are built into the app and do not need a connection.

Backups

When you export a backup, Katahdin creates a JSON file and saves it wherever you choose. When you import one, the app reads only the file you pick.

Children

Katahdin collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

简要说明

Katahdin 不收集任何数据。没有账号，没有统计分析，没有广告，也不跟踪你。开发者没有服务器，看不到你的徒步记录。

你的徒步记录

你记录的徒步保存在你的设备上。如果为 Katahdin 打开了 iCloud，记录还会通过你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库同步到你的其他 Apple 设备。这些数据由 Apple 存在你的 Apple 账户下，开发者无法访问。Apple 如何处理 iCloud 数据，见 Apple 隐私政策。

可以在系统"设置"（你的名字 › iCloud）里关闭同步。要删除记录，可以在 App 里删除；或者删除 App，并在"设置 › 你的名字 › iCloud › 管理储存空间"里删除它的 iCloud 数据。

定位

只有在你点地图上的定位按钮时，Katahdin 才会获取你的位置，用来显示你在步道上的位置和最近的避难所。位置只在你的设备上、只在那一刻使用，不会保存，也不会发送给开发者或任何人。

地图

地图使用 Apple 地图。和所有显示 Apple 地图的 App 一样，你的设备会向 Apple 请求你正在查看区域的底图。步道路线和地点数据内置在 App 里，不需要联网。

备份

导出备份时，Katahdin 生成一个 JSON 文件，保存在你选择的位置。导入时，App 只读取你选中的文件。

儿童

Katahdin 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

变更

如果本政策有变化，新版本会发布在本页，并更新生效日期。

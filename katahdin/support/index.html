Common questions

How do I log a hike?

In the Hikes tab, tap +. Pick the start and end from the list of shelters, gaps, campsites, trailheads and parking areas, type a mile, or pick a point on the map. You can also press and hold the trail on the map: set a start, press and hold again, set an end.

Why didn't my total go up by the full length?

Sections that overlap ones you've already logged only count once. The form shows how much of a new section is new.

My hikes aren't syncing between devices.

Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud is on for Katahdin (Settings › your name › iCloud). Sync can take a few minutes. The Settings tab in Katahdin shows the current iCloud status.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. The trail, places and your progress are built into the app. Only the Apple Maps background needs a connection, so it may be blank offline.

How do I move my data from the Trail-Tracker website?

On the Trail-Tracker website, click Export JSON and save the file somewhere your iPhone can see it, such as iCloud Drive. In Katahdin, go to Settings › Backup › Import backup and pick the file. Hikes are merged by id, so importing the same file again never creates duplicates. To go the other way, Settings › Export backup writes a file the website can import with Import JSON.

Where do the miles come from?

The trail is scaled to the Appalachian Trail Conservancy's 2026 official length of 2,197.9 miles. Miles in the app are approximate; always carry a current guide and map.

Is this an official app?

No. Katahdin is not affiliated with the Appalachian Trail Conservancy or the National Park Service. Trail data: NPS & ATC; places © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); state boundaries: US Census Bureau.

常见问题

怎么记录一段徒步？

在"记录"页点 +。起点和终点可以从避难所、垭口、营地、步道入口、停车场的列表里选，也可以输入英里数，或者在地图上选点。也可以直接在地图上按住路线：设为起点，再按住一处，设为终点。

为什么总里程没有增加整段的长度？

和已有记录重叠的部分只算一次。记录表单里会显示这一段里新增了多少。

记录在设备之间不同步。

确认两台设备登录的是同一个 Apple 账户，并且为 Katahdin 打开了 iCloud（设置 › 你的名字 › iCloud）。同步可能需要几分钟。Katahdin 的"设置"页会显示当前的 iCloud 状态。

没有信号能用吗？

能。步道路线、地点和你的进度都内置在 App 里，只有 Apple 地图的底图需要联网，离线时可能是空白。

怎么把 Trail-Tracker 网页版的数据搬过来？

在 Trail-Tracker 网页版点导出 JSON，把文件存到 iPhone 能看到的地方，比如 iCloud 云盘。然后在 Katahdin 里打开"设置 › 备份 › 导入备份"，选中这个文件。记录按 id 合并，同一个文件导入两次也不会重复。反过来也可以：在"设置"里点导出备份，得到的文件能在网页版用导入 JSON 导入。

里程是怎么来的？

路线按 Appalachian Trail Conservancy 2026 年公布的官方全长 2,197.9 英里换算。App 里的里程是近似值，徒步时请携带最新的指南和地图。

这是官方 App 吗？

不是。Katahdin 与 Appalachian Trail Conservancy 和美国国家公园管理局没有任何关联。步道数据：NPS 和 ATC；地点：© OpenStreetMap contributors（ODbL）；州界：美国人口普查局。
